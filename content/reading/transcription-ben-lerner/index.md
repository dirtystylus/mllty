Fun to read because some of the events take place in Providence, RI, where I spent a few summer breaks during college.

Reminded me a bit of Susan Choi’s *Trust Exercise* in terms of how it looks at the slipperiness of memory and narration. Who can claim authorship over an event? Did things actually happen to me, or am I remembering someone else’s story?
