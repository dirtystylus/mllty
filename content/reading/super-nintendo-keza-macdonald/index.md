Traded off reading chapters from this with my eldest. I no longer play games regularly, but my kids are in the sweet spot for Nintendo — they have the Switch 1 and 2 consoles, and are deep into the history of the games (especially the Zelda series). 

I’ve long admired Nintendo’s approach to making games: they way they focus on the *feel* of the characters as a starting point, the way they build their teams with experienced staff working alongside newer designers/programmers, the way they lean on principles instead of chasing trends. Feels like an antidote to today’s slop ’n ship ethos.
