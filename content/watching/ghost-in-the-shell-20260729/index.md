Another packed screening at Bryn Mawr Film Institute. I really should stop thinking I can just roll up 15 minutes before showtime — the line stretched out the door. The visiting lecturer who introduced the film drew some really interesting historical connections between the growth of science fiction in Japan to the themes of the movie, to our current moment where we are embodying many of the notions of a cyborg, just by being so plugged into a vast pool of readily-available information. (I just wish they hadn’t misgendered the Wachowski sisters — unintentionally, I hope — when talking about the film’s influence.)

This movie is so familiar to me on a sensory level. So many of the shots have been copied elsewhere, as homage or plagiarism. The score absolutely rocks — when those first drumbeats hit with the credits I levitated out of my seat. You can see the influence of _Blade Runner_ in the rainy cityscapes, but I also thought a lot about Michael Mann’s films (the neon aesthetics but also how the gunfire packs a wallop in his sound mixes).

The plot, though? I forget it as soon as the credits roll.
