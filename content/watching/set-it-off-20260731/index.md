The story beats to set up the quartet’s turn to bank heists are fairly predictable, but the leads have such charisma that you go along for the ride. Queen Latifa blows the doors off as Cleo (loved the insistence on a proper soundtrack when boosting a car).

The three decades gone by have really made the sums of money in this sound silly. Would like to watch this and _Widows_ as a double-header.
