Watched this with S, who was seeing it for the first time — she really enjoyed it, and her enthusiasm made me appreciate it more. We broke it up over two evenings, which helped because I still feel like this movie is too long. The Tau Ceti sequences are some of my favorites and I kept wanting to get there faster.

Rocky was a hit with S, puppets were a great decision. Kudos to Gosling and James Ortiz for building real emotional weight to their shared performances.

Love the score, at times it reminded me of the synth score from _Industry_, especially with Ken Leung in the cast. Still wish they had given him more to do.
