Feels like it will take a turn into horror but instead ends up somewhere funnier and sadder. More of an ache than a visceral gut-punch due to the quiet approach and the reserve in Beer’s and Auer’s performances. (I had forgotten that Auer was in Petzold’s _The State I Am In_, years ago.

I think that the Nina Hoss collaborations still hit linger in my mind more than these recent ones, but that’s partly due to the rush of discovering a filmmaker for the first time.
