So stylistically heightened in the first hour or so — the score, the performances, the camerawork — that I found myself sweating (it did not help that it was a warm theater). Everyone in the foursome is capital-C committed. The final third successfully navigates a turn into quieter, more introspective waters, and gets to some real emotional depth. Olivia Wilde, comeback kid.
