Was supposed to see this in 70mm but BMFI has been having issues with their projector. Didn’t really matter — we sat pretty close to the screen and it was plenty immersive anyway.

Nolan’s films don’t always excite me — I think he often tries to be too clever, and I wish he would collaborate with a writer who can make dialogue sing. But they’re always well-made, and with this one you can feel the budget in every frame. The action stuff vanished from my brain pretty rapidly, what stuck instead was a handful of scenes: Circe and the pigs, Sinon in Hades, and the Cyclops.

I highly recommend [Angelica Jade Bastién’s appearance](https://play.cdnstream1.com/s/kcrw/the-sam-sanders-show/does-the-odyssey-have-a-0175bf) on the Sam Sanders Show — she better articulates some of my issues with the film, dropping absolutely golden one-liners along the way.
