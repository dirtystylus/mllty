Not sure I’ve seen this since it was released. Felt like it was from the 70s on this rewatch, so much paranoia in every frame. The way Spielberg uses the camera to situate characters in time and space never fails to amaze me. I’ve heard criticism of Bana in the lead role and I do agree that it’s not the best performance in the film, but it doesn’t ruin it for me. I forgot about the final shot, but oddly appropriate given the 25th anniversary of 9/11 just two days earlier.

Blood for blood, over and over.
