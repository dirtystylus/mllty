Working through some of my gaps in the Petzold filmography, after watching _Miroirs no. 3_. Who wouldn’t instantly ruin their life for Nina Hoss?

I’m at an age where Ali’s character hit me the most: his alienation from his wife, his childhood home country, his adopted country. He has a line late in the film that explicitly expresses all those feelings, and I really felt that. A man longing for home, now that it is impossible to have. The ending feels like Greek tragedy: three lives ruined by fate.
