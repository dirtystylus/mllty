_Project Hail Mary_ but make it horror. What a fascinating cast. Hiroyuki Sanada as the captain makes so much sense…just the coolest cat flying into the face of the sun. Post-Johnny Storm, pre-Captain America Chris Evans — that early, cocky persona really got turned into something else for the MCU years. I thought this was one of Rose Byrne’s earliest roles until I checked her credits and this was her 18th (!) film credit (she stayed in the Boyle/Garland fold with= _28 Weeks Later_ this same year).

I had forgotten this was an Alex Garland script until the final act, when things get hallucinatory and weird and bloody. A little glimmer of catharsis at the end, repeating the most evocative line from the script in case you missed it earlier.
