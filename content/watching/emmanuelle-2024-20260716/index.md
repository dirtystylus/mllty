I haven’t seen the original, so I can’t comment on how much this echoes or subverts it. The _Babygirl_ comparisons hold in some respects — but if _Babygirl_ creates tension from the possibility of public shaming due to one’s kink breaking containment, this one just…floats. It drifts its fingers on the surface of some very rich possible explorations: sex as labor, sex as refuge from labor, sex as fun. I think there’s too many ideas and not enough heat, and I wish that balance had been calibrated better.

Will Sharpe is very interesting as a kind of Ghost of Sexless Future, narcotized by climate change-ennui, and when he and Noémi Merlant share scenes you can see how this might have been a corporate thriller-tinged _Before Sunrise_.

Naomi Watts is mostly wasted. Someone like De Palma (or Lynch!) would have some psychosexual mirroring going on between the two beautiful women. Dark/light bobs. Naomi/Noémi, just make that text. Instead you get that dynamic between Emmanuelle and the Zelda character played by Chacha Huang.

Still, this reminded me that I haven’t seen Audrey Diwan’s _Happening_, nor Merlant’s _The Balconettes_.
