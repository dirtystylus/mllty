Still as goofy and charming as ever. Watched this with S and she was howling at Fran’s makeover/transformation — what, these glasses? Don’t actually need ’em.

You can see Baz working through some of the visual language that would go supernova in _Romeo + Juliet_ and _Moulin Rouge_. Really glad this got a 4K restoration and forthcoming disc release, because the transfer currently streaming on Prime Video is pants.
