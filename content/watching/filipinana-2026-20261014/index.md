Caught this at the Film Forum during a work trip to NY. They were playing old-timey Filipino ballads in the pre-show. I read that this originated as a short, and at times I feel like the feature length is working against it. Very good compositions (I love a still camera). Loses things a bit when it tries to tie together colonialism, greed, and tragedy — the final scenes are supposed to jolt you out of the soporific pace, but I’m not so sure it was successful. Still: a treat to hear my paternal grandparents’ dialect (Ilokano) onscreen.
