Watched this as part one of a double-header with _Dark City_. Gets darker and sadder every time I see it. Interesting to see this now that we’ve had a generation or two of kids who grew up with their lives broadcasted on YouTube and other social media platforms. Today the casual betting on the show’s events would be done on Kalshi.

Even when Laura Linney plays a villain (I saw her recently in _The House of Mirth_) I can’t hate her. Her “Mococoa!” and “It’s unprofessional!’ line readings have been seared in my brain since I first saw them. This time through I noticed how often she’s looking off to a camera, expressing discomfort or outright begging for help.
