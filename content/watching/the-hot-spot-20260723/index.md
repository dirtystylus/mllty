Noir is often about how sex scrambles the brains of men who overestimate their intelligence, so there’s a certain comfort to the familiar way that things play out here. The plot matters less than the atmosphere conjured out of dust, sweat, and beautiful women. I wished for an ending that played more like _Bound_ instead of what we got, but overall this worked for me.
