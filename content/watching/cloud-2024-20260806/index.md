The first half is slower but I liked it more, and I preferred the slow dread to the second half’s madcap, over-the-top violence that felt like a throwback to the 90s when everyone was doing a Tarantino bit.

You try to earn a little pocket money by scamming people and end up (figuratively? literally?) in hell.

Not sure I’ll revisit this anytime soon, but I am looking forward to Kurosawa’s upcoming samurai film.
